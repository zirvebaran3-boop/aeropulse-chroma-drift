AeroPulse: Chroma Drift is a soft-colored, one-tap arcade flight game.

Tap to keep your ship in the air and slip through crystal obstacles. The closer you graze them, the more energy you collect. When your energy is full, fire Pulse and smash through everything in your way.

LEVELS
• Travel through five worlds, from Cyber Azure to Quantum Matrix

SHIPS
• A hangar full of ships: classic drones, rockets, UFOs, butterflies, dragons and more

GAMEPLAY
• One-tap controls — anyone can play instantly
• Zen, Arcade and Overclock difficulty modes
• Graze combo system and Pulse ability
• Procedural synth music and sound effects

PRIVACY
• No ads
• No account, login or internet connection required
• No personal data is collected; your scores stay on your device
