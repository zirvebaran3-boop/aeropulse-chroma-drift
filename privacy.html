Privacy Policy & Support

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no accounts, logins, ads, analytics or tracking, and it never sends gameplay data to a server.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, collected prisms, unlocked ships, language and sound settings are stored only on your device. They are never shared with us or third parties, and they are removed when you delete the app or choose "Reset saved scores" in settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children.
